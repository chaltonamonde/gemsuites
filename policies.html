Transparent Guest Policies

Clear, plain-language guidelines designed to ensure mutual respect, safety, and flexible hospitality for every resident and visitor.

1. Arrival & Departure Timings

Check-In: 14:00 • Check-Out: 11:00

Early check-in from 09:00 and late check-out up to 16:00 may be arranged subject to suite availability upon request. Direct bookings receive priority consideration.

Guests arriving before their suite is ready are warmly invited to relax at the Argenti Lounge or securely store luggage with our reception concierge.

2. Flexible Direct Cancellation Policy

We understand that international flight schedules and executive itineraries shift. Our direct cancellation policy is simple and fair:

  • Hotel Suites (Short Stay): Full refund or free cancellation with at least 48 hours notice prior to 14:00 on scheduled arrival date.
  • Extended Stay Apartments (14+ Nights): Written notice of 7 calendar days required for full refund of unused nights.
  • Modifications & Credits: Cancellations made within the notice window can be credited toward a future stay within 12 months at no penalty.
  • Direct Handling: No automated cancellation fees. Simply send a WhatsApp message to our reservations team to adjust your dates.

3. Housekeeping & Residential Services

For our One-Bedroom and Two-Bedroom Serviced Apartments, deep housekeeping, linen refreshment, and kitchen sanitization are conducted twice weekly, with daily trash clearance and evening turndown.

Additional customized daily housekeeping schedules or private chef arrangements via Argenti can be tailored to resident preference.

4. Swimming Pool & Wellness Safety

  • The heated outdoor pool is open daily from 06:30 to 19:30.
  • Children under 14 years of age must be accompanied by an adult guardian at all times in the pool area.
  • No glassware is permitted along the poolside deck; shatterproof drinkware is provided by Argenti.

5. Indoor Non-Smoking Environment

To safeguard air quality and comfort for all residents, GemSuites Riverside is strictly non-smoking in all indoor suites, hallways, and elevators.

Smoking is permitted exclusively on open private balconies, the Rooftop Sky Lounge terrace, and designated outdoor courtyard gardens.

6. Privacy & Data Protection

We treat guest information with the highest degree of confidentiality in full compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act and international privacy standards.

Guest names, contact numbers, and itinerary details collected via our website forms or WhatsApp are used solely to confirm reservations and deliver personalized hospitality. We never sell or distribute guest data to third-party marketing brokers.
