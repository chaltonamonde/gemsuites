Our Philosophy

The Art of Quiet Luxury

Conceived as an urban retreat along Riverside Lane, GemSuites unites the personalized privacy of high-end serviced residences with the thoughtful services of a boutique luxury hotel.

The Destination

Crafted for Serenity, Focus, and Living

GemSuites Riverside was created for travelers who seek refined substance over ostentation. Situated in the embassy and executive corridor of Riverside, Nairobi, our property provides a peaceful residential sanctuary shielded from urban bustle.

Whether staying for a three-day diplomatic delegation or six months on international assignment, our hotel suites, one- and two-bedroom apartments, and Presidential Penthouse deliver seamless acoustic privacy, gourmet kitchens, and dedicated high-speed fiber connectivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about stays, dining, conferencing, and direct reservations.

GemSuites Riverside is situated along Riverside Lane in the leafy, diplomatic suburb of Riverside, Nairobi, Kenya. It is approximately 5 to 7 minutes from Westlands and 25 to 35 minutes from JKIA Airport via the Nairobi Expressway.
We feature Luxury Hotel Suites for short executive visits, fully furnished One-Bedroom and Two-Bedroom Serviced Apartments with full chef's kitchens and in-unit laundry, and an expansive top-floor Presidential Penthouse with wraparound rooftop terraces.
Our property features Argenti Restaurant for contemporary all-day dining, our panoramic Rooftop Sky Lounge for sunset cocktails, the restorative Infinity Spa, an outdoor heated swimming pool, and an executive fitness gym.
We host executive gatherings and conferences from 10 to 180 guests in versatile layouts including the Plenary Ballroom, Riverside Executive Boardroom, and our indoor-outdoor Garden Terrace.
Direct bookings can be submitted via WhatsApp or online email form with real-time rate estimates. Payment can be settled securely via Lipa na M-Pesa (Paybill / Till), credit/debit card, or bank transfer upon arrival confirmation.
